Early Days of Babar Azam

Babar Azam was born on October 15, 1994, into a Punjabi Muslim family in the walled city of Lahore, Pakistan. He grew up in the neighborhood of Firdous Colony, where his early passion for cricket was nurtured alongside his cousins, Kamran Akmal, Umar Akmal, and Adnan Akmal, who later became established international cricketers.

Fascinated by their success, Babar started playing street cricket before joining the Gaddafi Stadium as a ball boy in 2007 to closely watch international players. His formal cricket journey began at the Zaheer Abbas Academy, followed by his inclusion in the Lahore under-15 team.

Recognizing his immense potential, his father, Azam Siddique, supported his ambitions by taking him to trials and purchasing his first cricket kit. Babar quickly progressed through Pakistan's domestic age-group ranks, representing the country at both the 2010 and 2012 ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cups, where he emerged as Pakistan's leading run-scorer and laid the foundation for his future international stardom.
