Babar Azam initiated his One Day International (ODI) career on May 31, 2015, making his debut against Zimbabwe at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore, where a breakthrough half-century instantly marked him as Pakistan's next marquee white-ball batsman.

Across 143 ODI matches, he has amassed 6,626 runs at an extraordinary, world-class batting average of 53.44. Functioning as the elite anchor of the top order, he has built a towering legacy highlighted by a career-best individual score of 158 against England.

Renowned for his surgical precision and signature cover drives, Babar holds prestigious historical records, including hitting three consecutive ODI hundreds on two separate occasions and becoming the fastest batsman to cross 5,000 ODI runs. His sustained statistical dominance ensured a historic, extended reign as the ICC World No. 1 ranked ODI batter.
