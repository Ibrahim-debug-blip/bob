Babar Azam launched his List A career on February 10, 2010, debuting for Pakistan Customs against ZTBL in Multan, and subsequently forged one of the most statistically dominant 50-over domestic and international profiles in modern cricket history.

Across 211 matches—encompassing 143 One Day Internationals (ODIs) alongside high-profile domestic limited-overs fixtures—he has amassed a world-class batting profile. Known for his incredible consistency in pacing 50-over chases, he etched his name as the first Pakistani batter to cross the milestone of 30 centuries in List A cricket.

His career-best score of 158, combined with a robust strike rate of 85.78, underlines a stellar run-scoring graph for regional heavyweights like the Allied Bank Stallions and State Bank of Pakistan that seamlessly fed into his legendary reign as the ICC's World No. 1 ranked ODI batter.
