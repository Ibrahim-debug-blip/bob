Babar Azam launched his Test match career on October 13, 2016, making his debut against the West Indies in a day/night Test match in Dubai, where he distinguished himself as the first player in cricket history to score a debut fifty under lights.

Across 62 Test matches for Pakistan, he has accumulated 4,481 runs at a solid batting average of 42.68, functioning primarily as the technical cornerstone of the national top order. His international pedigree is headlined by an epic, career-best individual score of 196 against Australia at Karachi in 2022.

Known for his serene patience and text-book strokeplay, Babar ascended to a peak ICC career ranking of World No. 2 in the longest format. He led the red-ball side as Pakistan's Test captain in 20 matches, instilling stability during a transitional era before navigating a highly publicized, challenging form slump in recent multi-day cycles.
