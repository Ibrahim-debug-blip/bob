Babar Azam initiated his first-class (FC) career on December 10, 2010, representing National Bank against ZTBL in Sialkot, establishing a solid technical foundation that would underpin his long-format international success.

Across his multi-day career—which comprehensively encompass Pakistan's premier domestic Quaid-e-Azam Trophy fixtures alongside 62 Test matches for the national side—he has built a foundational matrix of 12 centuries and 43 half-centuries.

Representing prominent domestic outfits such as the Zarai Taraqiati Bank, Sui Southern Gas Corporation, and Central Punjab, Babar honed an exceptional top-order technique that saw him reach a peak career ranking of World No. 2 in Test cricket, solidifying his stature as one of Pakistan's most accomplished multi-day anchors.
