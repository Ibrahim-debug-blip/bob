Babar Azam commenced his T20 International (T20I) career on September 7, 2016, against England at Old Trafford, quickly evolving into one of the most prolific anchoring batsmen in the history of international short-format cricket.

Across 145 T20I matches for Pakistan, he has accumulated 4,596 runs at an impressive batting average of 38.95. His exceptional ability to control the innings from the top order is highlighted by 39 half-centuries and 3 centuries, featuring a career-best score of 122 against South Africa.

As a tactical pillar of Pakistan’s top order, Babar became the fastest batsman to reach several run milestones in T20Is and has frequently held the ICC World No. 1 ranking. He heavily influenced Pakistan's modern era by captaining the side in 85 matches, registering 48 victories and guiding them to the final of the 2022 ICC Men's T20 World Cup.
